# The Way of Grace

*The nuns taught us there were two ways through a market. The way of nature. The way of grace.*

*Nature only wants to please itself. The way of grace does not try to please. It accepts being forgotten. Accepts being wrong. Accepts the loss.*

*This is a curriculum in the way of grace.*

---

A companion to the [trading project](https://github.com/5x5x5x5/trading). Each lesson arrives at something concrete — a metric, an instrument, a piece of code you can run. The arc is the same as its sibling, [quant-lessons](https://github.com/5x5x5x5/quant-lessons). The voice is not.

*We start with what we can measure.*

1. **[The measurable](measurable/returns.md)** — returns, volatility, the random walk.
2. **[The contract](options/contracts.md)** — a promise you need not keep.
3. **[The Greeks](greeks/delta.md)** — how the door moves, and its hinge.
4. **[The surface](vol-surface/implied-vol.md)** — what the market says about tomorrow.
5. **[The engine](regime/market-makers.md)** — who stands opposite each trade.
6. **[The measure](backtest/metrics.md)** — did it work, and what does that mean.
7. **[The lesson](ml/labeling-problem.md)** — what is worth learning from noise.
8. **[The flows](flows/microstructure.md)** — ticks, events, cross-asset currents.

*Begin where everything begins — with a number that moved.*
